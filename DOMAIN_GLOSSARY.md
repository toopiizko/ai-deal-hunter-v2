# Domain Glossary

- **Product:** normalized device or item model.
- **Listing:** one seller's offer for a product.
- **Deal Score:** explainable 0–100 estimate of deal quality.
- **Market Price:** comparison benchmark; mock data in Phase 1.
- **Fair Price:** estimated condition-adjusted value; mock data in Phase 1.
- **Risk Score:** scam or listing warning assessment, separate from Deal Score.
- **Watchlist:** locally saved listings or product targets.
- **Price Alert:** condition for notifying when price reaches a target.
- **Sold Alert:** future notification when a listing becomes unavailable.
- **Source:** original marketplace or listing platform.
- **Seller:** person or business offering a listing.
